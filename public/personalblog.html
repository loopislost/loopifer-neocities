📪 Rambling about Bookblog(Sept 11 2026 14:30)

One of my roommates has Covid I think. After all these years maybe I'll finally get it. Anyhow, I started on Dune: Messiah (finally!). I'm realizing that I kind of liked Speaker for the Dead better than it? So I might switch some numbers around. God... assigning ratings is so hard. I know on the actual page I go on and on about how I'm going to actually use all the numbers or whatever but I still think the vast majority of my ratings are still from 5-9. Maybe if I got into fanfic we could drop those numbers a lot more. I don't actually want to subject myself to books I wouldn't enjoy though? That may be a flaw in my own idealized view of the system. I've stumbled on a few books I was compelled to rank terribly but for the most part I try to choose books I think I would like. And now that Remain has put me off straightforward romance that's cutting off a whole (very large) genre. Though then again I really liked Pixels of You which was pretty romance-y. I don't fully understand the rationale behind my own opinions half the time. There are a LOT of popular ships that I don't like and a lot of mediocre popular ships that I do like. I may have to accept my hypocrisy and move on at this point.
