100 Webmaster Questions

1. Please introduce yourself.
Hi, I usually go by Loop/Loopislost online. Nice to meet you!
2. How long have you been making websites?
About 3 months!
3. And what got you into the hobby?
I saw a couple of youtube videos about neocities several years ago and thought the idea of it was cool but didn't actually start on it until over the summer while I had way too much time on my hands.
4. What kind of website are you most interested in?
For websites by other people: I like the ones that have a ton of depth and pages that you can find if you spend a lot of time clicking around.
5. What's your workflow? Do you plan your websites out thoroughly or do you come up with the design as you go along?
I have vague ideas of what I want to do but those ideas only solidify when I'm in the process of actually making decisions. A lot of the visual design of my website was made through trial and error.
6. Please link to your biggest inspirations.
Todd in the Shadows(youtuber music critic, love him).
7. What's your favourite part about making websites?
I like seeing everything come to life after I've worked out all the kinks. When I can look at a webpage and it feels finished and cohesive it makes me very happy.
8. And the thing you struggle with the most?
Hmmm. I'm not sure. I'll come back to this question later.
9. Do you keep the same layout on all of your pages? Or do you use different ones?
Visually I think my site stays fairly consistent but pages do have different layouts. It just depends on what my amount of css knowledge was at the time of making the page.
10. How confident are you with CSS?
I haven't even started looking into CSS animations, so there's a lot to learn! I'm pretty confident with CSS that does not involve animations at least.
11. Do you know how to correctly use <dl>?
I'm going to be honest, I had no idea what it was before doing this questionnaire.
12. What is your favourite HTML element?
The first thing that comes to mind is <details> because that's the last thing I was working on before this. In all honestly, I don't think I have a real favorite? It's never occurred to me that I should pick one.
13. If you're making a new web page from scratch, what is the first thing you do?
I use an SSG (Eleventy) to build my site, so I just set the custom title/header text. It makes everything so nice and simple! SSGs are pretty cool, even if you're managing pretty well without them they let me do a ton of neat stuff with auto-building my website.
14. Do you know JavaScript?
I know enough to do the things I want to with my website. From the small amount of experimental coding I've done, it doesn't seem terribly different from Python, which i do have experience with.
15. How about PHP?
I know nothing about PHP, other than the fact that it had a huge security risk a couple days ago.
16. Does your website have a theme that you stick to?
Yes! Orange!
17. Are you more focused on content or design?
I started out more focused on design, but I'm happy enough with the design now that all I have to do at this point is fill out more pages.
18. Do you own a domain name? If not, would you ever want to?
I do not own one, and I don't care enough. This is just a silly personal website so I don't need anything crazy. Maybe one day I'll migrate off of Neocities but I don't see that happening in the near future.
19. What do you think of nostalgia-focused or "retro" websites?
People can do what they want. I don't care too much either way.
20. Is your HTML valid? Do you even check?
I haven't used an actual checker... I think everything should be good? I try my best to make sure I close all my tags.
21. What are your opinion on buttons and banners?
With too many buttons/banners it's easy for things to get lost or feel overwhelming. I don't focus too much on collecting buttons/banners for my own site but having just a few more would be cool.
22. What do you think of button walls in particular?
Like I said earlier, can be overwhelming. If they are things about the user it can be fun to learn about them through those buttons though.
23. If you started over again, would you make something similar or completely different?
It would be pretty similar. My website is very much made to suit my own aesthetic tastes and I can see myself going down pretty much the exact same path of design decisions.
24. Are you envious of other people's websites?
I was at first... A lot of people have really awesome websites with so much stuff going on and they feel so personal and quirky and creative! Especially in comparison to mine which feels so bog-standard. Nowadays it doesn't bother me at all-- I like my website the way it is!
25. What text editor do you use?
VSCode.
26. Why do you use that one?
I had to use it when I took a python class and it's convenient. Had to turn all the annoying AI features off but other than that it has served me well. I would definitely recommend over the neocities native editor... figuring out how to set up the github pushes is annoying but you only have to do it once ever and then everything works seamlessly. I followed Petrapixel's Git Tutorial to link everything up.
27. Do you host your image files on your web server, or on another host?
The vast majority are just hosted on my site. My button is linked on catbox.moe (in case it gets hotlinked enough to matter... mmm very optimistic of me I know)
28. This might not be relevant to you, but what's your opinion on the Neocities vs. Nekoweb debate?
I'm not sure if the Nekoweb culture is really my thing but I am happy that we have multiple options out there.
29. How much server space would you estimate your main website takes up?
Last I checked I was using up ~2% of my allotted space on Neocities, so around 20MB? I have no idea I may be stupid
30. Do you keep local backups of your files?
Yesyesyesyes! Cannot recommend enough. I followed Petrapixel's Git Tutorial to set up my VSCode and Github. Losing all these files would be devastating and nothing except for some horrible disaster can take them away from me now.
31. Do you prefer simple or highly visual websites?
A lot of the time less is more, but I do like when websites have a bit of edge to them. The scrappiness of the indieweb is part of its appeal. However, a couple of the websites out there really scream "graphic design is my passion" and, well, not in a good way.
32. Do you stick to certain colours? Do you do that on purpose, or is it your subconscious?
Yeah, fuck ton of orange and yellow/cream. Orange is so cool. And purple. Around half of my Animal Jam Play Wild animals have the orange/yellow/purple color scheme. I have no idea why I love it so much.
33. Have you ever thought about quitting? Why?
Running this thing is more fun than stressful, so not really. I wouldn't really "quit", per say, I would probably just forget about this place. Hoping that doesn't happen though!
34. Do you have many webmaster friends, or is it a solitary hobby?
Ehhh not really. I follow a handful of people on Neocities but I don't really know them. And my irls aren't quite as interested in all of this as I am.
35. Do people in your real life know about your website?
Speaking of IRLs-- Yeah, a couple do. My partner says he reads a lot of the stuff on here and I've briefly showed it off to a couple of people. I generally try to refrain from posting anything I wouldn't want them to see because of that.
36. Do you update your website very often? How often is "very often"?
I update somewhere between every couple of days and multiple times a day. I've been surprisingly consistent, considering how busy I've been lately.
37. And the overall design, do you change that much? Why or why not?
I don't think my index page has gone through any changes other than tiny tweaks. The first iteration of it was already good enough for me. Same story with most of the other pages.
38. Is your website more you-focused, hobby-focused, or outside world-focused?
Hobby focused/me focused. I mean a lot of it is the book reviews... and a fair amount is my thoughts on things I really like.
39. Do you do web design professionally?
Nope!
40. If not, would you like to? And if you're comfortable answering, what do you do for work?
I think I have a cousin who does. The field seems pretty competitive. I'm possibly too confident in my graphic design skills so I think I would do a good job with the visual part but I haven't thought much about doing that. Currently, my only jobs are being a student and working at a call center.
41. Do you communicate with people by email very much?
I ignore my email as much as I can get away with doing so. So, no.
42. Some people reject social media and use websites as a replacement. Do you keep social media outside of your website?
Sadly I am not free from the chains of social media. I've been introduced to RSS Feeds and RSS Readers through my time on the indie web, and those are a good supplement to my social media but I can't fully replace all my brainrotted scrolling. I have been reading a lot more, which helps!
43. How about instant messengers? Do you use a mainstream one like Discord or Telegram? Or something like Matrix? Do you avoid them?
I'm active (ish) on Discord. I know it's a bad company (and it just keeps getting worse!) but I haven't felt a big enough push to jump ship yet (plus a ton of my school clubs use Discord as their main source of communications).
44. Do you listen to music while you work on websites? If so, what kinds of artists?
I do listen to music. Um. Several kinds.
45. Do you keep everything you make on one website, or do you have more than one?
Just the one. I don't think I'll get broad enough in scope of content to want to or need to make a second.
46. On a similar note, do you keep to one topic on your site, or many?
The article section is just whatever I feel like talking about. Not too many topics currently, but I hope to cover many more... at some point.
47. Do you present your real self, or at least try? Or do you construct a persona on purpose?
I don't think too hard about the division between my "real self" versus my persona. I think I used to have more of a fake persona when I was younger online, but a large motivation for that was repressed gender dysphoria which I have partially resolved (by being the gender I want to be irl)
48. Have you ever made a good friend thanks to your website?
Not really? I like seeing what the people I follow on Neocities post but I'm not really friends with any of them.
49. Are you happy with the way HTML and CSS currently work?
They seem to work fine. I'm happy.
50. What are practices that you think people should avoid?
All the classics: inline css, low contrast, not keeping backups.
51. What about under-utilised practices, or things you think people should do more?
I can't think of anything, sorry :(
52. Do you use a lot of semantic HTML? Or are you guilty of generic structure?
I do whatever I learned works first, and only change if I figure the impact is big enough for me to do so. I'm not really clear on what semantic HTML is but I probably use a bit of semantic and generic (??)
53. Do you consider different browsers?
I don't actively think about them much. I do want my website to look nice on all browsers though.
54. Speaking of, what's your preferred browser? Convince your readers why they should use it.
I use Firefox on my PC and Brave on my phone. Firefox is just... a lot better than Chrome (fuck that), which is a lot better than Edge or whatever. I use Brave on mobile mainly because of the built in ad-blocking. As I've recently discovered, both Firefox and Brave are great for blocking trackers and cool things like that! If you want to know how trackable you are, check out this website.
55. And what OS are you on?
Debian on my PC, Kubuntu on my school laptop, and Android on my phone. Yeah, I'm one of those Linux people. Sue me. (I'm just a larper dw I have no idea what I'm doing)
56. Do you have a strong opinion on that, or do you just happen to use it?
Windows is disgusting and I generally dislike Microsoft and all of its products. I continually resent having to use Microsoft products for all schoolwork ever (I want to stab excel to death). Open source stuff is pretty cool, but you have to be more patient and more willing to spend hours researching stupid issues.
57. Are your websites mobile-friendly?
Last I checked, all the pages look pretty good on mobile!
58. What are your thoughts on autoplay?
Please don't.
59. What are your thoughts on webrings? Are you in any?
I am not in any... I would like to be, I just never get around to it. I'm a little too involved with working on my own website to mess around with the social stuff as much.
60. Do you have any web shrines? What do you like to see in that sort of page?
I have a page dedicated to my old Minecraft Worlds if that counts? Idk, if you're passionate/care a lot about something you should write about it! It's fun.
61. Are your websites "cliche", in your opinion?
Possibly??
62. What is your ideal website? Are you striving for that, or for something else?
My ideal website is just my current one, but with even more stuff and looking even more lived in.
63. Are you an artist? Do you draw or design your own assets?
I am an artist! I have drawn almost every artwork on this website (except for some buttons and the cats on the index page). There's not a ton of random images around the site though.
64. What are your favourite resource sites?
I have genuinely used Petrapixel's website for so much stuff. Her tutorials have been very handy.
65. Is there a habit you just can't get away from no matter how hard you try?
I can't think of any bad habits. In terms of website design at least? In terms of real life it would probably be procrastinating.
66. What's your biggest advice for a new webmaster?
Don't compare yourself too much to others I guess. Don't make it into a chore, do it because you enjoy it.
67. Do you keep all your styling in CSS? Or do you hard-code some?
What does "hard-coding" your styling mean??
68. What do you think of frameset layouts?
I don't know what those are.
69. How about table-based layouts?
I started off only using flexbox but table based layouts are so helpful.
70. Do you subscribe to the ideas of "one-column", "two-column" and "three-column" layouts? Do you use any of these?
A handful of my pages are 2 column, quite a few are 1 column, and my index page is just a mess. So no, I wouldn't say I "subscribe" to any of those ideas.
71. Do you spend longer on the HTML or the CSS?
At this point, the HTML. When I first started, the CSS.
72. Have you ever made a page with no CSS? It's useful for your thoughts.
No, and I'm not sure why I would want to.
73. Do you ever find yourself making layouts with nothing to put on them? Or do you only make layouts when the need arises?
No, I make layouts while I make the contents of the page.
74. Would you consider yourself a beginner? Or advanced? Somewhere in the middle?
Probably intermediate. There's a lot I don't know but I'm confident with static sites at the very least.
75. Do you have a habit of looking at the source code of websites you visit?
I do very rarely (if there's a specific element I'm curious about). Generally looking through source code is just confusing.
76. How did YOU learn how to make websites?
I started off with the Neocities Tutorial and everything else I've learned has been through W3Schools, Stack Overflow threads, or other people's websites.
77. Do you ever force elements to do things they're not supposed to?
No, elements were built the way they were for a reason...
78. Thoughts on floating elements?
Haven't messed with them much, if at all.
79. When you're sizing stuff, what do you use first? Do you use px, em, %, or something else?
I used %, sometimes px for the margins. I know using em is better overall for accessibility but I didn't realize that until way later and I don't feel like it's a big enough issue to warrant going back and changing things.
80. Do you have a favourite font?
Courier New, the font the main text of my website uses, is pretty cool. I also like the wacky fonts I use as headers, though those could be obnoxious if not used sparingly.
81. Would you run a website with another person? How would that work?
I would be down for that. It would just be like any other group project, but hopefully about something we both care about.
82. Do you surf the Web to find new personal websites very often?
I browse Neocities sometimes to find exciting websites, not often. Outside of that, it is shocking to me how frequently I search up a question and there's an answer from some tiny personal blog. I wouldn't have given that a second thought before.
83. Do you bookmark other people's websites? How would you feel knowing someone else bookmarked yours?
If they have RSS feed's I'll follow them (pssst.... I have an rss feed too) or I'll follow their Neocities account. I would be honored if someone thought my website was interesting enough to want to bookmark.
84. What do you want people to be most impressed with when they see your website?
I don't think people would find the look terribly impressive, but I do hope that people notice the randomized cloud backgrounds. I still love those.
85. Are you interested in technology outside of websites? Do you collect?
I don't care too much about older technology. I have a Barnes & Noble Nook from like 2014 if that counts for anything, and my cell phone is a model from 2016.
86. How often and for how long are you online?
When I don't have school to occupy my time, it's all the time. During school it's just tiny bits sprinkled throughout the day.
87. When it comes to your website, who is your target audience?
People who are interested in media criticism I suppose. Or just some rando's thoughts online.
88. Have you ever been interested in XHTML?
Not really. I'm not sure what it could offer me to make it more interesting than normal HTML.
89. Do you program in general? Have you ever written a program for use with or on your website, not counting simple JavaScript?
I took AP Computer Science Principles in Highschool and another basic programming class in college. I'm pretty well versed in basic Python stuff at this point. I also wrote my own custom quiz using JavaScript which I have yet to publish on this website. I kind of want to use it for data collection which I obviously can't do here.
90. Speaking of programs that help you make websites, what do you think of static site generators (SSGs)? Have you ever used one?
I currently use Eleventy, and it's pretty nice! Painful to migrate when you already have quite a few pages, but once you do it's sweet.
91. Do you keep a hitcounter? Why or why not?
I have one of the F2Clap widgets. I could probably add a visitor counter... It's just not at the forefront of my mind RN.
92. Do you frequent forums? Which ones?
I used to use 16Personalities WAY back in the day but I haven't been on any real forums since. Reddit, I guess.
93. Do you write your page content directly into the editor, or do you prepare it elsewhere, like a text document or a Word document?
I write it directly into the editor. If I were to prepare it in a text document I would just use a .txt, and I would still use VSCode to edit it so nothing would be different.
94. Do you think you appear cool to others? A more accurate answer now: do other people ever say you're cool?
I asked my pet and he said No.
95. Are you embarrassed of your old work? Have you ever deleted everything out of shame?
I have SO MUCH bad writing from elementary/middle school. If I wrote it long enough ago I just think it's funny but the more recent stuff is mortifying. I suffer too much from embarrassment.
96. Would you close down your website if you couldn't update it, or would you leave an archive?
I don't see any reason to not leave it up. I think there's enough worthwhile content on here that others might get something out of.
97. Do you reveal a lot about yourself on your website? Or are you more secretive?
I guess I don't get super personal on here. I try to follow basic internet safety stuff (no real name, no precise location) because you have no idea who could be out there reading this. I'm not that secretive though.
98. Are you willing to reveal who your best online friend is, and/or if they have a website?
Nah
99. And do you optimise the images on your website?
I try to keep file sizes small but other than that no.
100. We're out of time! How do you feel after answering 100 questions? ....other than exhausted.
I'm glad I got this over with, now back to real life! ! !
